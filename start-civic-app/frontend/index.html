How the AI helps

Four automated layers, one accountable pipeline

Every report flows through detection, scoring, dispatch, and verification — visible to citizens, officials, and workers in real time.

1

Duplicate detection

New reports are matched by location and description similarity. Matches merge into crowd confirmations instead of cluttering the queue.

2

Priority scoring

Issues near schools and hospitals, with more confirmations or longer wait times, automatically rise to Critical or High.

3

Heat map & dispatch

Officials see hotspots live on a map and assign the nearest available worker to a region in one click.

4

Verified resolution

Workers submit after-photos; citizens confirm the fix before a case closes, with points and titles awarded on both sides.

Public portal

Report a civic issue

Upload a photo with embedded GPS or a visible GPS Map Camera Lat/Long stamp. CivicPulse reads the coordinates and pins the location automatically.

📷 Choose a GPS-tagged / GPS Map Camera photo

JPEG, PNG or WebP, max 8 MB. Upload an original camera photo with GPS metadata, or a clear GPS Map Camera photo displaying “Lat 13.033376° Long 80.180313°” (example). Coordinates are read automatically by OCR.

What happens next

1. We scan open reports within ~120m using the same category and similar wording.

2. A match merges your report in as a crowd confirmation — no duplicate ticket, and the issue's priority rises.

3. New issues get an AI priority score based on category severity, proximity to schools/hospitals, and how long they've waited.

4. Once a worker marks it resolved, you'll be asked to confirm with a rating before it's closed.

The pin shows GPS coordinates from EXIF metadata or the visible GPS stamp in your photo. Printed stamps can be edited, so this does not guarantee the true location. Manual map selection is disabled.
